FluentMint

A software income stream for course creators and educators.

A partnership offer where FluentMint builds, hosts and supports software for educators and the educator keeps 50% of every sale. It targets course creators in fields such as tax, bookkeeping, podcasting and therapy.

Visit fluentmint.com

Design decisions

  • A pale mint hero (a light green wash) with a dark teal headline (#0B3F47) in Manrope and body copy in Source Sans 3 (#3B4A52).
  • The hero pairs the headline with a "The partnership" card split into You bring, We bring and You keep rows, so the deal is readable at a glance.
  • Audience pages are linked as a list of educator types (Tax, Bookkeeping, Podcast, Legal, Therapist, Speech therapy), each with a one-line pitch.
  • The FAQ is an accordion of eight questions with plus and minus markers; several can stay open at once.
  • A small team section shows three named people with round photos and their roles, and a WhatsApp button sits in the corner.
