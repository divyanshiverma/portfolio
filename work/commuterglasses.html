Commuter Glasses

Photo-led pre-launch page built around one $1 reserve button.

A pre-launch page for sunglasses that float an ebook in front of the eye. A $1 reservation holds a pair at 20% off.

Visit commuterglasses.com

Design decisions

  • The hero is a full-bleed dark photo of a Kindle beside a glowing green projected page, so the product idea is shown before it is explained. The headline sits in the dark upper left, clear of the glow.
  • Type: Archivo set very large in white over the photo, with a widely spaced capital wordmark and a short nav of Features, FAQ and Reserve.
  • One conversion action: a filled white 'Reserve for $1' button with the terms right beside it ('$1 holds your pair. 20% off at launch.'), echoed by an outlined 'Reserve' in the nav and repeated further down the page.
  • After the hero the page switches to white feature sections: a portrait photo on one side and a large headline on the other ('Looks like sunglasses. Reads like a Kindle.'), with plain short paragraphs.
  • The deposit goes through a standard Stripe checkout, so the payment step is familiar and the amount stays small.
  • Mobile: the nav keeps only the wordmark and the Reserve button, the button grows to a full-width-feeling block, and the photo sits below the copy so the green page stays in view.
