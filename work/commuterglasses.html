Commuter

Sunglasses that show your book.

A pre-launch page for sunglasses that float an ebook in front of your eye. The goal is a $1 reservation that holds a pair at 20% off.

Visit commuterglasses.com

Design decisions

  • Full-bleed dark photo of a Kindle next to a glowing green projected page, so the product is shown before it is explained.
  • Archivo set very large and white over the photo, with a spaced-out wordmark and a plain nav of Features, FAQ and Reserve.
  • One conversion action repeated: "Reserve for $1" in the hero, in the nav, in the feature stories and at the foot, with a waitlist email field as the fallback.
  • The $1 reservation opens a Stripe checkout for "Commuter Glasses Early Bird Reservation", so the deposit is small and the payment step is familiar.
  • The page has no dark mode, only the photo-led dark hero.
