Forget the Tools

Price-anchored landing page for a $99 research service.

A $99-a-month service that sends marketing research reports on ads, Google search, ChatGPT recommendations and creators to Shopify founders and DTC brands.

Visit forgetthetools.com

Design decisions

  • The hero is built around a price anchor: a white card of tool rows, each with its logo, its job and a red struck-through monthly price, ending in a dark green 'Research done for you $99' bar. The rows sit at slight angles, like a pile of receipts.
  • Colour: a chartreuse (#D2E823) hero with deep green (#254F1A) headlines in a very heavy Schibsted Grotesk, and near-black (#1E2330) for the nav button. Every section after it changes background, so the page reads as distinct blocks while scrolling.
  • Pill tags above the headline name the audience and the offer before the headline does: 'For Shopify founders', 'For DTC brands' and '$1,306 of tools for $99'.
  • A dark green ticker under the hero scrolls tool logos with struck-through prices, repeating the anchor at the fold.
  • Service table: columns for 'What you want', 'What you get' and 'Tools you skip'. Each row leads with a plain outcome in heavy type, gives one concrete example in the middle, and ends with the logos and price it replaces. Four rows show at first, behind a 'Show more' toggle, followed by 'All ten for $99 a month.' and the CTA.
  • Pricing section: a maroon block with a pink '$1,306 a month for apps. 44% of a $3K store.' headline beside an itemised bill of eleven tools. Two cards below set a card statement (-$1,306, in red) against monthly orders (+$2,940, in green).
  • Navigation: a floating white pill nav with dropdowns and a theme switch next to the dark CTA. On mobile it collapses to a 'Menu' pill, the hero stacks into one column and the CTA goes full width.
