forgetthetools

Done-for-you marketing research for Shopify and DTC brands, $99 a month.

A subscription service that delivers marketing research reports on ads, Google search, ChatGPT recommendations and creators. It is aimed at Shopify founders and DTC brands who cannot justify a stack of research tools.

Visit forgetthetools.com

Design decisions

  • The hero sets the price anchor immediately: a card of tool names with struck-through monthly prices (Foreplay $59/mo, Modash $299/mo and so on) ending in a dark green "Research done for you $99" bar.
  • Bold chartreuse (#D2E823) background with deep green headline text (#254F1A) in Schibsted Grotesk at a very heavy weight, with a dark #1E2330 body colour for the nav and buttons.
  • A scrolling dark green ticker of tool logos with struck-through prices runs under the hero, repeating the anchor.
  • The pricing section switches to a maroon block with a large pink "$1,306 a month for apps. 44% of a $3K store." headline, a line-item table of tools and a card-statement versus orders comparison.
  • The service list collapses behind a "Show more" control; each row pairs a plain benefit with the tool logos and the monthly price it replaces.
  • On mobile the nav becomes a "Menu" pill and the hero stacks into one column with a full-width CTA, and a dark theme exists that inverts the palette.
