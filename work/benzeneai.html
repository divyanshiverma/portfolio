Benzene

Small models. Stable bonds.

The site of Benzene, an independent AI lab. It presents itself like a page from a chemistry journal.

Visit benzeneai.com

Design decisions

  • A scientific-figure look: a labelled benzene ring with "Fig. 1, benzene C6H6", a 1.39 angstrom bond dimension and a pi-cloud callout inside a dotted compass circle.
  • Libre Caslon Display for the huge "Benzene" wordmark and Libre Caslon Text for captions, in ink blue (#243E52) on pale blue-grey.
  • The ring can be dragged to turn it in 3D, with the hint "Drag to turn" in italic beside the control.
  • "Fuse a ring" adds a ring and relabels the figure (Fig. 3, phenanthrene C14H10), so the one control shows the brand idea of stable bonds.
  • Corner crop marks and a single email link keep the page quiet.
