Benzene

AI lab site laid out like a figure in a chemistry journal.

The site of Benzene, an independent AI lab.

Visit benzeneai.com

Design decisions

  • The page is laid out like a figure plate: a line-drawn benzene ring inside a dotted compass circle, with tick marks at the quarters, crop marks in the page corners and a caption in journal style, 'Fig. 1, benzene C6H6'.
  • The drawing uses technical annotation as decoration: a π callout on a leader line, a 1.39 Å bond dimension with end ticks, shaded electron clouds and a dashed axis through the ring.
  • Type: Libre Caslon Display for the large 'Benzene' wordmark and Libre Caslon Text for captions, in ink blue (#243E52) on pale blue-grey. Italics mark the figure number and the 'Drag to turn' hint.
  • Interaction: the ring can be dragged to turn it in 3D. 'Fuse a ring' adds a ring, fills the button dark and relabels the caption with the new molecule's name and formula, so one control carries the 'Stable bonds' idea.
  • The layout stays sparse: the wordmark and tagline bottom left, the figure on the right, and one email link top right as the only navigation.
  • Mobile: the figure moves to the top at full width, with the caption and controls under it and the wordmark last.
