About

I work across product and brand, which on a small team means I do both at once: the logo and the checkout, the illustration and the empty state. I care about the parts that make a site feel made by a person, like a plate you can build, a lamp cord you can pull or a colour swatch that redraws the garment.

My own site, divyanshiverma.com, has my marketing work and my blog. Behance has longer case studies.
